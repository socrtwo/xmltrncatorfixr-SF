XML Truncator-Fixer PWA

Repair corrupt XML, including document.xml parts of broken Office Open XML files (docx, xlsx, pptx). Runs entirely on your device — your files are never uploaded. Archives are unpacked with the in-house Immortal Inflater, a fault-tolerant decompressor that recovers partial data from truncated or corrupt files instead of failing.
